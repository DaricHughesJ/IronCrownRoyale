# Iron Crown: Medieval Battle Royale

A 3D medieval battle royale built in **Unity 6 (6000.6.4f1)** with 25, 50, and 100 combatant lobbies, bot AI, Dwarven Sky-Galleon airship drop, canvas glider mechanics, and hybrid medieval combat.

## Features

- **25 / 50 / 100 Combatant Lobbies**: Configurable garrison sizes with intelligent bots (Squire, Knight, Warlord).
- **Dwarven Sky-Galleon Airship Drop**: Leap from a high-altitude flying warship, freefall, and deploy a canvas glider over the island.
- **1200m x 1200m Island & 6 Medieval POIs**:
  - Highkeep Citadel (central royal fortress & great keep)
  - Oakhaven Hamlet (cottages & windmill)
  - Ironforge Watch (dwarven garrison & smithy)
  - Ravenmire Abbey (gothic ruins)
  - Kingsport Docks (harbor & cog ships)
  - Sunken Stones (monolith ring)
- **Hybrid Medieval Arsenal**:
  - Melee: Oaken Cudgel, Arming Sword, Royal Halberd, Sunforged Greatsword
  - Ranged: Hunting Shortbow, War Longbow, Siege Arbalest, Dwarven Chu-Ko-Nu Repeater
  - Tactical Throwables: Francisca Throwing Axes, Wildfire Alchemical Flasks
  - Alchemy: Healing Poultices, Draughts, Elixirs
  - Armor Progression: Tier I Leather, Tier II Chainmail, Tier III Gilded Full Plate
- **The Cursed Blight**: 5-stage shrinking storm ring with damage per second.
- **Cross-Platform PC & Mobile Controls**: Full keyboard/mouse controls with pointer lock, plus an on-screen dual-virtual-joystick and touch action button overlay for mobile.

## Controls

### PC (Keyboard & Mouse)
- `W A S D`: Move
- `Left Shift`: Sprint
- `Space`: Jump / Dive from Airship / Deploy Glider
- `Mouse Move`: 360° Camera (Escape unlocks cursor)
- `Left Click`: Attack / Fire Ranged Weapon
- `Right Click`: Aim Zoom / Raise Shield Block
- `1`, `2`, `3`, `4` or `Scroll Wheel`: Weapon Slots
- `E` / `F`: Loot Pickup (auto-loots arrows and armor upgrades)
- `H`: Quick Heal Potion
- `V`: Toggle 3rd Person / 1st Person Helmet View
- `M` or `Tab`: Toggle Full Tactical Map

### Mobile Touch HUD
- Left Virtual Thumbstick (outer ring triggers sprint)
- Right-side screen drag for camera look & aim
- Action buttons: Attack/Fire, Aim/Shield Block, Jump/Glider, Loot, Quick Heal

## How to Run

1. Open **Unity Hub**.
2. Click **Add -> Add project from disk** and select this folder.
3. Open `Assets/Scenes/IronCrownBattleRoyale.unity`.
4. Press **Play** in the Unity Editor.
5. To export a standalone `.exe`, use the top menu: `Iron Crown -> Build Standalone Windows (.exe)`.
